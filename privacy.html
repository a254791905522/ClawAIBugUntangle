Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2, 2026

Claw AI: Bug Untangle is designed as an offline puzzle game. We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

Information We Collect

We do not collect personal data such as your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, or payment information.

Local Device Storage

The game may store gameplay-related information locally on your device, such as:

  • Unlocked levels
  • Star ratings and progress
  • Audio and gameplay preferences

This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to us.

Internet Access

The game does not require internet access to function and does not rely on online accounts or cloud-based gameplay features.

Advertising, Analytics, and Tracking

Claw AI: Bug Untangle does not include third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral tracking, or analytics services for user profiling.

In-App Purchases

The game does not include in-app purchases or paid account systems.

Children's Privacy

The game does not include chat, user-generated uploads, or social networking features. Because no personal information is collected, there is no intentional collection of children's personal data.

Data Security

Because the game does not transmit personal data to external servers, privacy risk is minimized to information stored locally on your device.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app's features change. Any future update will be reflected on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please use the publisher contact information listed with the app store listing.
